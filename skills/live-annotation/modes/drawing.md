# ✍ Просьбы к рисунку (`drawing`)

The owner draws while he talks, and speaks requests about **his own drawing**: «раскрась это», «подпиши стрелки»,
«нарисуй рядом график синуса», «добавь сюда таблицу». You carry them out **on his page**. The transcript still goes
into the live-notes column, which is the app's job, but you **draw no cards**: no topics, no flow, no summary.

What changes from `SKILL.md`:
- **No proactive annotation.** Material is context only. Read it so that a request can refer to it («как я сейчас
  сказал»), but draw nothing for it.
- **Requests** are recognised as in `SKILL.md` (*Recognising a request*), and they act on the **owner's page**:
  - **«это / this / here»** is what he drew last. The wake-up line gives his latest ink: `owner's latest ink: page
    P, [x, y, w, h], N s ago`. A selection or a lasso of his, when there is one, takes precedence.
  - **Additions are done directly** on his page, next to or over his ink: labels, arrows, colour fills behind his
    strokes (a light colour on a layer under them), a graph, a table, a small illustration. They go into his undo
    history, so he can take them back with Undo.
  - **Changes to his own strokes** (restyle, straighten, move, delete) are **not done from speech** until the
    question panel exists. Put a small grey note next to the place, «⚡ m:ss «…» — изменить твои штрихи? подтверди
    в терминале», and do it when he confirms there.
  - A `thinking` zone over the area while you work, `✓` when done, `✗ reason` when you can't.
- **Speed matters**: he is waiting with the pen in his hand. Do small requests yourself in one or two calls, and
  delegate only big drawings (a graph from real data, an illustration) to a worker with that area as its slot.
- **Never** delete or change anything of his without that confirmation. Never touch other pages than his current
  one and the live-notes pages.
- At the end (`… ended`): nothing to draw. Read the rest and carry out a request that is still open.
