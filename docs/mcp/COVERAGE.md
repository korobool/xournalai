## Main menu

197 menu entries, 197 with an action; every entry can be chosen by path with `ui_menu_select` (visibly, level by level) and is listed by `ui_menu_tree` (with shortcut, enabled and checked state). Entries without an action are filled at run time (recent files, toolbars, plugins) and are operated with `ui_inspect` / `ui_interact`.

## Dialogs

Any open window is listed by `ui_windows`, its widgets by `ui_inspect` (stable ids, labels, values), operated with `ui_interact` / `ui_keys`, seen with `ui_screenshot`.
